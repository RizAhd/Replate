How it works

Find a meal nearby, reserve it, collect it fresh.

  1. Find a meal nearby

    Browse surplus meals from hotels and restaurants around you, on a list or on the map.

  2. Reserve and pay

    Pick what you like, pay by card or cash on pickup, and get a QR code and a pickup code.

  3. Collect it fresh

    Show your code at the hotel during the pickup window and enjoy your meal.

Turn surplus into income

Every day, good food is thrown away because it could not be sold in time. List it on REPLATE and let nearby customers collect it.

  • List a dish in under a minute with a photo, price and pickup window.
  • Get notified the moment a customer reserves, and verify pickup by code or QR.
  • Reach new customers while reducing your food waste.
