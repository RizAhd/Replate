Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Draft for review. This is a plain-language description of what the REPLATE app does today. Have it checked and add your legal business name before publishing it on Google Play.

REPLATE connects customers with hotels and restaurants that have surplus food. This page explains what information we handle when you use the REPLATE app or the hotel and admin web panels.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and, if you set one, your pickup address and its map position.
  • Orders and activity: items you reserve, order status, pickup codes, reviews you write, favourites, and messages you send to a hotel.
  • Sign-in: if you sign in with Google, Facebook or a phone number, we receive the basic profile and identifier that provider shares with us.
  • Notifications: a device token so we can send order updates to your phone.
  • Diagnostics: anonymous crash reports and usage statistics (for example, which screens are used) that help us fix problems.

Your location

With your permission, the app uses your phone’s location while it is open to show nearby hotels and distances. Your live location is not stored on our servers. If you pick a pickup address, that address and its map position are saved to your profile.

Payments

Card payments are handled by our payment provider, PayHere. REPLATE never sees or stores your full card number. Cash on pickup is paid directly to the hotel.

Who sees your information

  • The hotel you order from sees your name, phone number, order details and chat messages for that order.
  • REPLATE administrators can see account and order information to run the service and resolve problems.
  • Service providers that run the app for us (Google Firebase for sign-in, database, storage, notifications and diagnostics; PayHere for card payments).

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account from the app (Profile → Delete account). This removes your profile and anonymises your past orders. Some records may be kept where the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Turn off notifications or location at any time in your phone settings or in the app’s Settings.
  • Clear the cached images and food lists from Settings → Data and privacy.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information using the contact details below.

Children

REPLATE is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@replate.app
