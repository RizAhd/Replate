# REPLATE hotel guide

This guide is for the hotel or restaurant staff who list surplus food on REPLATE and hand it to
customers. You work in the **hotel web panel**, a website you open in a browser on a computer or tablet.
Phones also work for scanning, but the tables are easier to read on a larger screen.

## 1. How it works

1. You register your hotel. An administrator checks your details and approves you.
2. You add the dishes you have left over, with a photo, a discounted price and a pickup time window.
3. Customers find them in the REPLATE app and place an order.
4. You see the order immediately, move it along (Confirm, Preparing, Ready), and when the customer
   arrives you scan their QR code or type their six-digit code to complete the pickup.

## 2. Registering your hotel

Open the panel and choose **Register your hotel**. Fill in:
- Hotel name and owner name
- Email and phone number (the phone number is what customers call)
- A password (at least 8 characters) and the same password again
- The hotel address, and the map position (latitude and longitude). Tap **Use my location** while you
  are at the hotel to fill the position in. Customers see your hotel on the map from these coordinates.
- Your **business licence** as a PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP file, up to 10 MB
- Tick the box to accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy

Tap the register button. You will be signed out and told that the registration was received. Your hotel
is **Pending** until an administrator approves it.

You cannot sign in until you are approved. If you try, the sign-in page says **Waiting for approval**.
If the administrator does not approve the registration, the sign-in page shows the reason instead. Once
you are approved you can sign in with the same email and password.

## 3. Signing in

Open the panel's **Sign in** page and enter your email and password. The Google and Facebook buttons
work for an account that is already linked to a hotel. **Forgot password?** emails you a reset link.
Phone sign-in is only in the mobile app.

The menu on the left (or the button at the top left on a small screen) has: **Dashboard**, **Add Food**,
**My Listings**, **Orders**, **Analytics**, **Chat**, **Settings** and **Logout**.

## 4. Dashboard

Four live numbers:
- **Total Revenue**: money from orders that were completed or delivered (not from orders still waiting).
- **Active Items**: dishes currently on sale.
- **Pending Orders**: every order that is not finished or cancelled yet.
- **Total Orders**: all orders.
Below them, **Recent Orders** shows the latest five; **View All** opens the Orders page. An **approval
badge** at the top tells you if your hotel is approved.

## 5. Adding a dish

Open **Add Food**.
1. Click the photo box and choose a clear, bright picture (JPG, PNG or WebP). It is shrunk automatically
   to a size that loads fast on mobile data.
2. **Food name**, **Category** (the list is managed by the REPLATE team), and a **Description**
   (ingredients, portion size, allergens).
3. **Original price** and **Discounted price** in LKR. The discounted price must be lower. The discount
   percentage is worked out for you.
4. **Quantity available**.
5. **Pickup start** and **Pickup end**: the time window when customers can collect it.
6. Click **Publish Listing**.

The dish appears in the app straight away and customers who follow deals get a notification. Only an
approved hotel can publish.

## 6. Managing your listings

**My Listings** shows every dish with its photo, prices, quantity and status.
- **Edit** opens a form with the name, category, description, prices, quantity and pickup times. You can
  choose a new photo, or leave it to keep the old one. Click **Save changes**.
- **Mark sold** takes a dish off sale (the app shows it as sold out); **Activate** puts it back.
- The bin icon deletes the listing after you confirm.
The quantity goes down automatically each time a customer orders, and the dish disappears from the app
when it reaches zero.

## 7. Orders

Open **Orders**. It has three tabs: **All Orders**, **Scan QR** and **Enter OTP**.

### All Orders
A table of your orders: the order number, the customer and phone, the items, the total, the review
(if any), the status and the actions.

Move each order forward with one button:

| Pickup orders | Delivery orders |
|---|---|
| **Confirm** | **Confirm** |
| **Preparing** | **Preparing** |
| **Ready** | **Dispatch** (asks to confirm) |
| **Done** (see below) | **Delivered** |

Every change sends the customer exactly one notification, so you never need to tell them yourself.
**View** opens the details. **Cancel** cancels the order after you confirm; the customer is told that the
hotel cancelled it.

An order shows **Slot ended** when the customer's pickup time (plus one hour) has passed.

### Completing a pickup: Scan QR
1. Open the **Scan QR** tab and allow the browser to use the camera (the site must be opened over
   HTTPS).
2. Point the camera at the QR code on the customer's phone.
3. A sheet shows the customer, the items and the total. Check them and press **Confirm** to complete the
   order.

### Completing a pickup: Enter OTP
If the QR will not scan, open **Enter OTP**, type the six-digit code the customer reads out, and press
the button. The same confirmation sheet opens.

### Expired codes
A code only works during the customer's pickup slot (one hour from the time they chose). If it has
expired you see a message and the sheet does not open. If you decide to hand the food over anyway, use
the **Done** button in the All Orders table.

## 8. Analytics

- **Revenue**, **Orders**, **Completion Rate** and **Active Listings** at the top; Revenue and Orders
  also show the change against last week.
- **Revenue (Last 7 Days)** is your daily earnings.
- **Order Status** shows completed, pending and cancelled.
- **Orders per Day (Last 14 Days)**.
- **Top Selling Items**: your most popular dishes.
- **Recent Orders**.
Revenue counts only completed and delivered orders.

## 9. Chat

Customers can message you from the app. Open **Chat**: the list on the left shows each conversation;
click one to read and reply. New messages appear instantly. Chat is only available to approved hotels.

## 10. Settings

- **Profile Information**: hotel name, owner name and phone number. Click **Update Profile** to save.
- **Address**: shown as read-only. It and the map position were confirmed at registration. To change
  them, contact REPLATE support.
- **Approval status**: Approved, or (if an administrator changes it while you are signed in) Pending
  approval, Not approved with the reason, or Suspended.
- **Sign Out**.
To change your password or email, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

## 11. Rules to remember

- Only list food that is safe to eat and fresh. The description should name allergens.
- Keep the quantity honest; it is what customers rely on.
- Set a pickup window you can really serve.
- An administrator can suspend an account. A suspended hotel is hidden from customers and cannot sign in
  until it is enabled again.

## 12. If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The camera does not start | Allow camera access in the browser, make sure the page is on HTTPS, or use the OTP tab. |
| A page sends me back to the dashboard | Your approval was withdrawn or your hotel was suspended while you were signed in. The message on screen says which; contact support. |
| A listing will not publish | Check your internet and that all required fields are filled; the page names the problem. |
| An order is missing | The table updates live; check you are signed in to the right hotel account. |
| I cancelled by mistake | A cancelled order cannot be reopened; ask the customer to order again. |

Support: support@replate.app
