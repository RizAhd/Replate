# REPLATE customer guide

REPLATE helps you buy good food from nearby hotels and restaurants at a discount, food that would
otherwise be thrown away at the end of the day. You reserve it in the app, pay by card or cash, and
collect it at the hotel by showing a QR code.

This guide follows the app screen by screen. Version 1.1.0, Android 7.0 or newer.

## 1. Before you start

- You need an Android phone with internet access. Allow **Location** so the app can show how far each
  hotel is, and allow **Notifications** so you hear when your order is confirmed or ready.
- You need an account. You can create one with your email, or sign in with Google, Facebook or your
  phone number.

## 2. First launch

1. The REPLATE logo appears for a moment.
2. Three short slides explain the app: **Discover Amazing Deals**, **Find Food Near You** and
   **Save Food, Save Planet**. Tap **Next**, then **Get started** (or **Skip**). You only see these once.
3. The **Login** screen opens.

## 3. Creating an account and signing in

The Login screen has two tabs, **Login** and **Register**.

**Register with email**
1. Open the **Register** tab.
2. Enter your name, email, phone number, a password and the password again. Each field is checked as you
   type.
3. Tap **Register**. You may be asked to verify your email: open the link we send, then log in. If the
   mail does not arrive, tap **Resend Email**.

**Log in**
- **Email and password**: enter them and tap **Login**. Forgot your password? Tap the reset link, enter
  your email, tap **Send**, and follow the email.
- **Google** or **Facebook**: tap the button and choose your account.
- **Phone number**: tap the phone button, enter your number, wait for the six-digit code in an SMS and
  enter it.

Your session is remembered, so you will not need to log in again the next time you open the app.

If you see **Account suspended**, an administrator has disabled your account. Contact support at the
address shown in Settings.

## 4. Finding food

The app has five tabs along the bottom: **Home**, **Map**, **Cart**, **Orders** and **Profile**. The menu
button (top left) opens a side menu with **Search**, **Notifications**, **Messages**, **Settings**,
**Help** and **Log out**.

### Home
- The top bar shows where deals are delivered to (your location) and a bell with a red number for unread
  notifications. If location is off, tap the bar to turn it on.
- **Search bar**: tap to open the full search screen.
- **Banners** at the top slide by themselves. Some open a category or a screen.
- **Browse categories**: tap a chip (All, Meals, Bakery and so on) to filter the list. The categories are
  set by the REPLATE team, so they can change.
- **Hot deals**: the biggest discounts right now, best first.
- **Recommended for you** appears once you have saved favourite dishes.
- **Nearby hotels and food**: every available dish, nearest first when your location is on.
- **Pull down** to refresh, or **shake your phone** twice. The app reloads the latest listings.

### Search
Tap the search bar on Home or open **Search** from the menu. Type a dish, a hotel or a cuisine. When the
box is empty you see your recent searches (tap one to repeat it, or **Clear** to remove them). If you have
not searched yet, the screen suggests the most popular categories instead.
Tap the filter button to **Sort by** nearest, lowest price, highest price, highest discount or top
rated, choose a **Category**, and switch on **Available items only**. Tap **Apply**; **Reset** clears the
filters.

### Map
The Map tab shows hotels with deals as markers around you.
- A banner at the top tells you if location or GPS is off, with a button to fix it. It updates the moment
  you switch GPS on or off.
- Tap a marker. A card slides up with the hotel name, address, how many deals it has and a preview of
  the dishes.
- **View deals** opens the search results for that hotel. **Show route** draws the walking or driving
  route on the map with the distance and travel time.
- The **heart** next to the hotel name saves the hotel to your favourites.
- Use the search box on the map to jump to a hotel or place.

## 5. A dish page

Tap any dish to open it. You see the photo, the original and discounted price, the discount badge, the
pickup window, how many are left, and how far away the hotel is.

- **Heart on the photo**: save the dish to Favorites.
- **Heart next to the hotel name**: save the hotel to Favorites.
- **Only N left**: dishes with three or fewer portions left carry an orange "Only N left" label on the
  card, so you know to act quickly.
- **Quantity**: use **-** and **+**. You cannot choose more than the hotel has left.
- **Add to cart**: puts it in your cart. A sold-out dish shows **Sold out** instead.
- **Call hotel**: asks for permission the first time, then calls the hotel. If you say no, the phone's
  dialer opens instead.
- **SMS hotel**: opens your messaging app with a message ready to send.
- **Chat with hotel**: opens a live chat inside the app.
- **Reviews**: read what others wrote. If you have completed an order for the dish, **Write a review**
  lets you rate it from one to five stars and add a comment.
- **Recommended for you**: other dishes from the same hotel.

## 6. Your cart

Open the **Cart** tab. Your cart is saved on your phone, so it is still there if your signal drops.
- Change quantities with **-** and **+**, tap the remove icon on an item to take it out, or tap **Clear all**.
- The summary shows the items, the total and **You save**.
- **One hotel at a time**: each order goes to a single hotel. If your cart holds dishes from two hotels,
  checkout stops and asks you to remove the extra items or order them separately.
- Tap **Proceed to checkout**.

## 7. Checkout

1. **Fulfilment**: choose **Pickup** or **Delivery**. Delivery adds a delivery fee of LKR 250 and asks for
   an address; type it or tap **Pick from Map**.
2. **Pickup time**: tap the field and choose when you expect to arrive. You cannot choose a time that has
   already passed. Your pickup slot lasts one hour from that time, and the QR code only works inside it.
3. **Payment method**: **Cash on Pickup** or **Card Payment (PayHere)**.
4. **Your details**: your name and phone number.
5. Tap **Place Order** and confirm.
   - With a card, the PayHere screen opens. The card details go straight to PayHere; REPLATE never sees
     them.
   - With cash, the order is placed straight away and you pay at the hotel.

When it works you hear a short sound and see **Order Placed!** with your QR code and a six-digit code.
A green line under the title tells you what the order does, for example "You are saving LKR 320 and
rescuing 3 meals from the bin".

## 8. Collecting your food

1. Open **Orders** and tap the order (or use the screen that appears after checkout).
2. The order screen shows the status, a **step timeline** (Placed, Confirmed, Preparing, Ready or On the
   way, then Collected or Delivered) that fills in as the hotel works, the hotel's address and your pickup
   time. A cancelled order has no timeline.
3. At the hotel, **show the QR code** to the staff. If the QR cannot be scanned, read out the six-digit
   **pickup OTP** instead.
4. The screen brightness rises by itself in a dark room so the code is easy to scan.
5. When the staff confirm, the order moves to **Completed** and you get a notification.

**Things you can do on the order screen**
- **Call Hotel** and **SMS Hotel** to ask about your order.
- **Navigate to hotel** opens turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps. **Open Pickup Route** shows the
  route in the REPLATE map.
- If the order is still pending you can cancel it from the list (**Cancel Order**).
- After it is completed you can leave a rating and a comment.

**If you are late.** The QR code and OTP stop working one hour after the pickup time you chose. The order
screen then says **Pickup time passed** and hides the code. Call the hotel to arrange a new time.

**If the hotel cancels.** The order shows **Cancelled by the hotel** and you are notified.

### Order statuses
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending Pickup / Pending Delivery | Placed, waiting for the hotel |
| Order Confirmed | The hotel accepted it |
| Preparing | The hotel is getting it ready |
| Ready for Pickup | Come and collect it |
| Out for Delivery / Delivered | Delivery orders |
| Order Completed | Collected |
| Order Cancelled | Cancelled by you or the hotel |

The **Orders** tab groups your orders into **Active**, **Completed** and **Cancelled**.

## 9. Favorites

Open **Profile**, then **Favorites**. There are two tabs.
- **Dishes**: the dishes you hearted. Swipe one away to remove it (tap **Undo** if it was a slip).
- **Hotels**: the hotels you hearted. Tap **View deals** to see their dishes, or the red heart to remove
  the hotel.
Favorites are stored on your phone.

## 10. Notifications and messages

- **Notifications** (bell on Home, or the menu): new deals, order updates and pickup reminders. Filter by
  **All, Orders, Deals, Pickup, System**; swipe one away to delete it, **Mark all read** or **Delete all**.
- **Pickup reminders**: about 30 minutes before your pickup slot starts you get one reminder with the
  hotel and the time, so you do not forget (turn this off under **Settings, Pickup reminders**).
- A notification in your phone's status bar has a **Mark as read** button.
- **Messages** (menu): your conversations with hotels. Tap one to continue chatting. Open a dish and tap
  the chat button to start a new one.
- Choose what you are told about in **Settings, Notify me about**.

## 11. Profile and settings

**Profile tab**
- Your photo, name and email, how many orders you have made and how much you have saved in total.
- **Your savings**: a bar chart of what you saved on your last six orders.
- Shortcuts to **My Orders**, **Favorites**, **Notifications**, **Settings**, **Help & Support**, **Logout**
  and **Delete Account**.

**Settings**
- **Profile information**: name, phone, email and pickup address (type it, or **Pick from map**).
  **Change photo** uses the camera or your gallery. Tap **Save changes**.
- **Notify me about**: switches for **Order updates**, **New deals nearby** and **Pickup reminders**, and a
  link to the phone's own notification settings.
- **Location**: managed in your phone's settings.
- **Help and about**: Help and FAQ, **Contact support**, **Rate REPLATE**, **Share REPLATE with friends**,
  and the list of open-source libraries.
- **Data and privacy**: what REPLATE stores about you, and **Clear cached images and food lists**.
- **Account**: **Log out**, and **Delete my account**, which removes your account and personal data
  permanently. Past orders are kept without your name so the hotel's records stay correct.

## 12. If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No deals on Home | Pull down to refresh; check your internet. If you are offline the app shows the last list it saved. |
| "Location is off" | Turn on Location in the phone settings, then tap the banner. |
| Card payment fails | Try again, or choose Cash on Pickup. If you were charged but see no order, contact support with the time and amount. |
| The QR will not scan | Raise the screen brightness and read the six-digit OTP to the staff instead. |
| No notifications | Allow notifications for REPLATE in the phone settings, and check the switches in Settings. |
| "Account suspended" | Contact support. |
| Forgot password | On the Login tab use the reset link and follow the email. |

Support: support@replate.app (also under Settings, **Contact support**).
